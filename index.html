CASE OVERVIEW

The Zodiac Killer was an unidentified criminal linked to attacks in Northern California during the late 1960s. The case became widely known through mysterious letters and coded messages sent to newspapers.

Confirmed Victims

Five people were killed in the four confirmed attacks.

Location

Northern California, USA.

Active Period

1968–1969 (confirmed attacks).

THE CASE TIMELINE

Explore the major confirmed events associated with the Zodiac Killer case.

20 DECEMBER 1968

LOCATION: LAKE HERMAN ROAD

David Faraday and Betty Lou Jensen were killed near Benicia, California. This is considered the first confirmed Zodiac attack.

04 JULY 1969

LOCATION: BLUE ROCK SPRINGS

Darlene Ferrin was killed and Michael Mageau survived an attack in Vallejo, California.

JULY 1969

EVENT: THE FIRST LETTERS

Newspapers received letters containing a mysterious 408-character cipher.

27 SEPTEMBER 1969

LOCATION: LAKE BERRYESSA

Bryan Hartnell survived an attack. Cecelia Shepard later died from her injuries.

11 OCTOBER 1969

LOCATION: SAN FRANCISCO

Taxi driver Paul Stine was killed. This became the fourth confirmed Zodiac attack.

DECEMBER 2020

EVENT: THE 340 CIPHER SOLVED

Independent codebreakers solved the famous 340-character cipher. However, it did not reveal the killer's identity.

LETTERS & SECRET CIPHERS

The Zodiac Killer became widely known for sending mysterious letters and coded messages to newspapers.

THE 408 CIPHER

In 1969, the Zodiac sent a 408-character coded message to three newspapers. Donald and Bettye Harden successfully solved it.

THE 340 CIPHER

Another famous message contained 340 characters. It remained unsolved for 51 years before being solved in December 2020 by independent codebreakers.

THE ZODIAC SYMBOL

The Zodiac used a distinctive crossed-circle symbol in his letters and communications. It became the most recognizable symbol associated with the case.

WHY ARE THE LETTERS IMPORTANT?

The letters helped investigators connect communications to the case. However, not every message attributed to the Zodiac has been authenticated.

THE UNSOLVED MYSTERY

Despite decades of investigation, the identity of the Zodiac Killer has never been officially established. The case remains one of America's most discussed unsolved criminal cases.
